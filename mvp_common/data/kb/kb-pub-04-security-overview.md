This page describes in general terms how Lumio protects customer data. It is a fictional summary for the demo.

## Data protection

Customer data is encrypted while it travels and while it is stored. Backups are kept for 35 days and are encrypted in the same way. Data is stored in the region chosen when the workspace was created.

## Access control

Staff access to customer workspaces is limited to what is needed for a support request and is recorded in an access log. Customers control who can join their workspace and which role each person has: owner, admin, member or guest.

## Sign-in protection

Lumio supports two-step sign-in for every account. Workspace owners can require it for all members. Repeated failed sign-in attempts lock the account for a short time.

## Reporting a security problem

If you think you found a weakness in Lumio, send a description through the contact form on the help site and choose the security topic. The security team replies within two business days. Please do not publish details before the problem is fixed.
