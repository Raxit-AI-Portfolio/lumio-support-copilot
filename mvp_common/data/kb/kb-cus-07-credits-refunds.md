If Lumio failed to deliver what you paid for, you can ask for a credit or a refund. This article shows how to ask.

## When you can ask

You can ask when there was a long outage, when you were billed twice, or when you were billed for a plan you cancelled in time. Requests should be sent within 60 days of the invoice date.

## How to ask

Open a ticket and choose the billing topic. Give the invoice number, the amount and the reason. A support agent reviews the request and collects the facts.

## What happens after you ask

A request does not give an answer at once. Money decisions are checked by a second person on the Lumio team before anything is paid out. You will get an update on the ticket when a decision is made. This usually takes up to three business days.

## Credit or refund

A credit lowers your next invoice. A refund returns money to the original payment method. Credits are applied faster. Refunds take five to ten business days to show on your statement.

## What is not covered

Unused seats in the middle of a month are not refunded. They appear as a credit on the next invoice instead.
