For Lumio support staff only. This article covers insults, threats and harassment in tickets and calls.

## What counts as abuse

Insults aimed at a person, threats, hate speech and repeated demands in capital letters after a clear answer. Frustration about a product problem is not abuse.

## Steps

### First message
Stay calm and polite. Reply once with a short note that you want to help and ask to keep the talk respectful. Do not argue.

### Second message
Send the standard warning text and tell the shift lead. Mark the ticket with the abuse tag.

### Third message
The shift lead takes over. The lead may move the contact to email only and may end phone contact.

## Threats

A threat of harm to a person goes to the security team at once, by phone, with the ticket number. Do not reply to the threat in the ticket until security says so.

## Protecting staff

An agent may stop working on an abusive ticket at any time and hand it over. No agent is required to continue a call after abuse. Offer the agent a break and the support wellbeing contact.
