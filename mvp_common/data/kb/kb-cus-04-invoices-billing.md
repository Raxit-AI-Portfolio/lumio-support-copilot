This article explains where to find invoices and how to change billing details.

## Finding invoices

Open workspace settings and choose Billing. The invoice list shows one invoice per month with its date, amount and status. Select an invoice to download it as a document file. Invoices are kept for seven years.

## Changing billing details

Owners and billing contacts can change the company name, billing address and tax number. Changes apply to future invoices only. Past invoices cannot be edited. If a past invoice is wrong, open a ticket and choose the billing topic.

## Payment methods

You can pay by card or by bank transfer. Card payments are taken on the first day of the billing month. Bank transfer invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Lumio retries after three days and again after seven days. You get an email each time. If the third try fails, the workspace becomes read only until the payment is made.

## Billing contacts

You can add up to three billing contacts. They get invoices by email but cannot change plans unless they are also owners.
