Lumio is a fictional project tracking service for small teams. This page gives a short tour of what it does.

## What Lumio does

Lumio lets a team plan work in boards, assign tasks to people, and follow progress in one place. Every board has columns, cards and due dates. Teams use it to run sprints, content calendars and simple support queues.

## Main features

### Boards and cards
A board holds cards. A card has a title, an owner, a due date, labels and a checklist. Cards move between columns by drag and drop.

### Reports
The reports page shows cards finished per week, overdue cards and the average time a card stays open. Reports can be exported as a spreadsheet file.

### Integrations
Lumio connects to calendars and chat tools so that due dates and mentions show up where people already work.

## Who uses Lumio

Lumio is built for teams of two to two hundred people. Plan tiers differ in storage, number of boards and support level. See the plan tiers article for the list.
