For Lumio support staff only. This policy says when and how to escalate a ticket.

## Levels

### Level 1
The first agent who takes the ticket. Handles how-to questions, account access and simple billing questions.

### Level 2
Senior agents. They take tickets that need log reading, data fixes or a policy exception.

### Level 3
Engineers on call. They take confirmed product defects and incidents.

## When to escalate

Escalate to level 2 when a ticket has no answer after two agent replies, when a customer asks for an exception, or when the ticket mentions data loss. Escalate to level 3 only after level 2 has confirmed the defect and attached the steps to reproduce it.

## How to escalate

Change the ticket owner to the level queue and write a handover note with the customer impact, what was tried and the exact question. Do not paste customer secrets in the note.

## Time limits

Level 2 must respond within two business hours for High tickets. Level 3 must acknowledge within 30 minutes for Urgent tickets, at any hour.
