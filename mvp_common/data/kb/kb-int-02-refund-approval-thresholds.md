For Lumio support staff only. This policy says who may approve a refund or credit, by amount.

## The two person rule

An agent never approves a request that the same agent prepared. The preparer records the facts and the amount. A different approver confirms the exact action before money moves. The system records the hash of the action that was approved.

## Thresholds

### Up to 50 dollars
One approver from the support team lead group is enough.

### From 50 to 500 dollars
The approver must be a support manager.

### Above 500 dollars
The approver must be a support manager and the finance duty officer must confirm as well.

## Limits per customer

No customer receives more than 1,000 dollars in credits in a calendar year without a written exception from the head of support.

## Evidence needed

Every request needs the invoice number, the reason code and a note that links the evidence: outage record, double charge or cancellation time. Requests without evidence are sent back.

## Refunds to a different method

Refunds go only to the original payment method. A different method needs finance approval.
