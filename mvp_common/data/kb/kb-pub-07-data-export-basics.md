You can take your data out of Lumio at any time. This page explains the basics.

## What can be exported

A workspace owner can export boards, cards, comments and attachments. Reports can be exported as spreadsheet files. Account sign-in records are not part of the export.

## How to start an export

Open workspace settings, choose Data, then choose Export. Pick the boards you want and the format: spreadsheet file or JSON file. Start the export and wait for the email that says it is ready.

## Time and size

Small workspaces are ready in a few minutes. Large workspaces can take up to a few hours. The download link works for seven days and then expires. You can start a new export at any time.

## After you leave

If you close a workspace, you can still export data for 30 days. After that, data is deleted according to the retention rules and cannot be restored.
