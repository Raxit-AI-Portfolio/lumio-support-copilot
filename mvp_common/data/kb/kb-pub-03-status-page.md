The status page shows whether Lumio is working. Check it first when something looks wrong.

## What the status page shows

The page lists each part of Lumio: web app, mobile app, API, file storage and email notifications. Each part shows one of three states: operational, degraded or outage. A history of the last 90 days is shown below the current state.

## Incident updates

When a part is degraded or down, the page shows an incident entry. The entry gets a first note within 30 minutes of detection and a new note at least every hour until the incident is resolved. After resolution a short summary is published within five business days.

## Subscribing to updates

You can subscribe with an email address to get a message when an incident starts, changes or ends. You can unsubscribe with the link in any message.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead. Maintenance windows are kept short and usually happen on Sunday early morning, Universal Time.
