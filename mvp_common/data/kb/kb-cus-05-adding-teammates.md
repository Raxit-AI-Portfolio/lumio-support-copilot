Owners and admins can add people to a workspace. This article shows how.

## Inviting people

Open workspace settings, choose People, then Invite. Type one or more email addresses and choose a role: admin, member or guest. Each person gets an invitation by email. An invitation stays valid for 14 days.

## Roles

### Admin
Admins manage people, boards and integrations but cannot change billing or delete the workspace.

### Member
Members create and edit cards on boards they can see.

### Guest
Guests see only the boards they were added to and cannot invite others. Guests do not count towards the paid user total on the Team tier up to five guests.

## Removing a person

Choose the person on the People page and press Remove. Their cards stay on the boards and show the name of the former member. You can reassign them to someone else.

## Seat count

The paid seat count follows the number of active members and admins. If you remove a person mid-month, the credit appears on the next invoice.
