Lumio is sold in three plan tiers. All prices are fictional examples for this demo.

## Free tier

The Free tier includes up to 3 boards, 5 users and 1 GB of file storage. Reports are limited to the last 30 days. Support is by community forum only.

## Team tier

The Team tier costs 8 dollars per user per month. It includes unlimited boards, 50 GB of storage per workspace, report history of 12 months and email support with a reply target of one business day.

## Business tier

The Business tier costs 15 dollars per user per month. It adds single sign-on, audit logs, 500 GB of storage per workspace and priority email support with a reply target of four business hours.

## Changing plan

A workspace owner can change plan at any time from the billing page. Upgrades start immediately. Downgrades start at the end of the current billing month. Data above the limits of the lower tier becomes read only until it is reduced.
