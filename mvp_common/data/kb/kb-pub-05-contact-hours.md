This page lists how to reach the Lumio support desk and when it is open.

## Support hours

The support desk is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 Universal Time. It is closed on weekends and on the fictional company holidays listed on the help site. Tickets sent outside these hours are answered when the desk opens.

## Ways to contact us

### Help site
Open the help site and use the contact form. This is the fastest way because the form asks for the details we need.

### Email
Write to support@example.com. Replies come from the same address. Please include your workspace name.

### Community forum
Free tier users can ask questions in the community forum. Staff read the forum on weekdays but do not promise a reply time.

## Reply targets

Reply targets depend on the plan tier. Team tier targets one business day. Business tier targets four business hours. Free tier has no target.
