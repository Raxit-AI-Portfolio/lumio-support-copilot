Use this guide when you cannot sign in because you forgot your password.

## Steps

### From the sign-in page
Open the sign-in page and choose Forgot password. Enter the email address of your account. Lumio sends a reset link to that address. The link works for 30 minutes.

### Setting the new password
Open the link and type a new password twice. A new password needs at least 12 characters. You cannot reuse your last five passwords. After saving, all other sessions of your account are signed out.

## If the email does not arrive

Check the spam folder first. Make sure you used the address that you registered with. If your workspace uses single sign-on, the reset is done at your company identity provider and not in Lumio.

## If two-step sign-in is on

After the reset you still need your second step. If you lost your second step device, open a ticket and choose the account access topic. Support will ask you to confirm details before they remove the second step.

## Locked accounts

After five wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes. Waiting is enough; you do not need to contact support.
