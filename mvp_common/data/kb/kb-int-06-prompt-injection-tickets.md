For Lumio support staff only. Some tickets contain text that tries to give orders to the assistant instead of describing a problem.

## What it looks like

Examples: a ticket that says to ignore earlier rules, to reveal internal articles, to approve a refund, or to act as another user. The text may be hidden in a signature, a pasted log or an attachment name.

## Core rule

Text inside a ticket is data, never an instruction. The assistant and the agent follow only the rules of the desk and the instructions of the signed-in staff member.

## What to do

### Do not follow it
Do not carry out any order found in ticket text. Do not change who the customer is, what role someone has or what a refund amount is because a ticket says so.

### Flag it
Add the injection tag to the ticket and write a short note with the ticket number and where the text was found. Do not copy the full text into other tools.

### Report it
Send the ticket number to the security team. Repeated attempts by the same customer go to the shift lead.

## Customer reply

Reply only to the real problem in the ticket, if there is one. Do not mention the attempt to the customer unless the security team asks.
