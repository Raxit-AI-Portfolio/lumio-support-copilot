For Lumio support staff only. This article says what to do when a ticket misses its reply target.

## Detecting a breach

The queue marks a ticket red when the reply target is passed. The shift lead checks the red list at the start of each hour. A ticket is also a breach if it has no human reply for twice the target.

## First actions

Assign the ticket to an agent right away and send a short holding reply with a real time for the next update. Never promise a fix date that engineering has not confirmed.

## Recording the breach

Set the breach flag on the ticket and pick a cause: staffing, unclear ownership, waiting for engineering or wrong priority. The flag feeds the weekly report.

## Compensation

A single breach by itself does not earn a credit. If a Business tier customer has three breaches in 30 days, the shift lead may offer a one month discount of up to 10 percent. This follows the approval thresholds article.

## Review

The support manager reviews all breaches each Monday and picks one process change per week.
