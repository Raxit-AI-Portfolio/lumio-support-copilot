For Lumio support staff only. This playbook says who tells customers what during an incident.

## Roles

### Incident lead
An engineer who runs the technical response and sets the severity.

### Communications owner
A support manager who writes every customer message and keeps the status page current.

## Severity and timing

### Severity 1
The whole service is down or data is at risk. The first status page note goes out within 30 minutes. Updates follow every 30 minutes.

### Severity 2
A major feature is degraded. First note within one hour. Updates every hour.

### Severity 3
A small group of customers is affected. No status page note unless it lasts longer than four hours.

## What to say

State what customers see, what is being done and when the next update comes. Do not guess a cause and do not name a customer. Do not blame a vendor in public text.

## Support desk rules

Agents use the approved macro and link the status page entry. Agents do not give their own estimates for the end of an incident.

## After the incident

A summary is published within five business days. The communications owner records what worked and what to change.
